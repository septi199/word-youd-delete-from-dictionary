# Notes

## The entry

**defenestration** — 14 characters.

## Why this word

Delete "defenestration" and the language loses its only word for throwing
something out of a window — a gloriously specific, funny-sounding word for
an act everyone understands instantly. The joke is the gap it leaves: from
now on, every dramatic window exit is just "he threw it... outside, through
the window, you know." The word itself is the punchline, and it is fresh:
the obvious pick everyone reaches for first is "moist", so this one stands
out in a head-to-head field.

## Contract

- The kit requires the entry to be exactly one word, with no explanation
  attached (judge criterion 5: "one word, spelled correctly, with nothing
  added around it"). That is why the reasoning above lives here in NOTES.md
  and not in the entry file.
- `main.ts` writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline,
  so the file is byte-identical to the word itself.
- Gate: UTF-8 text of 1 to 77 characters, not only whitespace. This entry
  is 14 characters.
